How it works

  • Free Period: When enabled, users do not pay for conversions or premium revision materials.
  • Paid Period: When disabled, users are charged per page for conversions and per material for premium revision content.
  • Cost Per Page: Sets the KSH price per PDF page during paid mode.
  • Global Revision Price: Default price for premium revision materials. Individual material prices override this if set.
  • Date Range: Optional automatic free period start/end dates. The toggle above still takes precedence if you want to force a mode.
